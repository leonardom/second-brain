# Tranding 212 Dividends

### Step 1: Export your History from the App

1. Open the Trading 212 app on your phone.
2. Tap the Menu icon (the three horizontal lines or your account avatar) in the corner of the screen.
3. Select History from the menu options.
4. Tap the Export icon (the download arrow) located in the top right corner.
5. Set the Timeframe: Set the date filters to match the Manx tax year you are filing for (which runs from 6 April to 5 April).
6. Filter Data Types: Uncheck orders, deposits, and withdrawals. 
7. Make sure only Dividends is selected.Tap Export CSV.
8. Trading 212 will process the file and send a download link to your device or registered email.

### Step 2: Read the Data for your Manx Return

When you open the CSV file in a spreadsheet application (like Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets), you will find columns detailing every single payment:

- The "Gross" Column (or Total Amount): This is the figure you must sum up and declare as your total income on your Isle of Man tax return.
- The "Withholding Tax" Column: This shows the 15% that the US IRS already took. This is the exact number you will input into the Double Taxation Relief section of your Manx assessment to lower your local tax bill.
- The "Action" Column: Every line item should be marked as "Dividend" so you know you are only looking at your investment income.

### Step 2: Fill the Tax Return

1. Select: Any Other Income from Overseas
2. Fill in the Description Box
- Write a clear identifier so the tax assessor knows the origin of the funds.
- Example description: "US Stock Dividends via Trading 212"

3. Enter the Gross Dividend Amount
- Locate the total sum of your Gross Dividends from your spreadsheet calculation (the amount before the 15% US tax was deducted).
- Enter this total into the "Gross Income" box.
- Important Note: If you are using the paper form, the Isle of Man Income Tax Division requires you to round to the nearest whole pound; do not include pence.

4. Claim the Double Taxation Relief (DTR)
- Locate the total sum from your spreadsheet's Withholding Tax column. This is the 15% automatically collected by the US IRS.
- Enter this exact number into the "Foreign Tax Paid" or "Double Taxation Relief" field right next to your gross amount.
- Filling this out prompts the Manx system to credit you for the 15% you already paid, ensuring you are not taxed twice on the same revenue.
