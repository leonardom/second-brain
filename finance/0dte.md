# 0DTE XSP Premium Selling Checklist

### 🛑 Pre-Trade Window (9:30 AM – 9:55 AM EST)
* [ ] **Check Economic Calendar:** Are there major market-moving events today? *(e.g., FOMC meetings, CPI data releases, or jobs reports)*. 
    * *Rule:* **If yes, do not enter trades before the data drops.** Wait until 15 minutes after the announcement.
* [ ] **Set Up Workspace:** Verify your Tastytrade 1x2 grid is active. Left side: **SPY 5-minute chart** (with VWAP and 9 EMA). Right side: **XSP 0DTE Option Chain**.

### ⚡ Entry Window (10:00 AM – 10:15 AM EST)
* [ ] **Determine the Trend:** Look at the SPY 5-minute chart on the left.
    * **Bullish Setup:** SPY is trading cleanly **above** its VWAP and **above** its 9 EMA. 👉 *Plan: Sell a Put Credit Spread.*
    * **Bearish Setup:** SPY is trading cleanly **below** its VWAP and **below** its 9 EMA. 👉 *Plan: Sell a Call Credit Spread.*
    * **Choppy / No Trend:** SPY is cutting back and forth through VWAP. 👉 *Plan: Do not trade. Close the platform and check back tomorrow.*
* [ ] **Select Strikes (XSP Option Chain):** Look at the right screen.
    * Find the strike closest to the **15-Delta** (.15). Click the **Bid** to sell it.
    * Buy the protection strike exactly **5 points further out-of-the-money** to lock in a $500 margin requirement.
* [ ] **Check Premium:** Verify the total net credit is between **$0.90 and $1.10**. 
* [ ] **Route Trade:** Send the order for **1 single contract** of XSP.

### 🛡️ Post-Entry Automation (Immediate)
* [ ] **Set the Profit Target:** The moment your order fills, immediately enter a **Buy to Close limit order at 50%** of the credit you received (e.g., if you collected $1.00, set the closing limit order to $0.50).

### 📈 Afternoon Management (1:30 PM – 4:00 PM EST)
* [ ] **Monitor Trend Breaches:** If your profit target hasn't hit yet, keep an eye on SPY.
    * **9 EMA Breach:** If SPY closes a 5-minute candle on the wrong side of the 9 EMA, monitor closely. If the spread value hits **2x your initial credit**, manually close for a controlled loss.
    * **VWAP Breach:** If SPY crosses the VWAP line against your position, **exit the trade immediately** at the market price to trigger your 3x hard stop loss. Never hold through a late-afternoon VWAP failure.
* [ ] **The 3:30 PM Rule:** If your spread is deep out-of-the-money (worth $0.01 to $0.05) but hasn't hit your exact 50% limit order yet, manually buy it back for pennies to entirely eliminate late-day "Power Hour" wildcard risk.
